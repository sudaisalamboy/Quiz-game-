About Trivia Master

Learn more about our quiz application and the team behind it

Our Mission

We believe that learning should be fun and engaging. Trivia Master was created to help people expand their knowledge in an entertaining way, challenging them with questions from various topics and difficulty levels.

Features

Our app offers thousands of questions from the Open Trivia Database, multiple categories, difficulty levels, timed challenges, progress tracking, and a competitive leaderboard to keep you motivated.

Technology

Built with modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, Trivia Master provides a seamless experience across all devices. We use the Open Trivia DB API to fetch fresh questions for our users.
